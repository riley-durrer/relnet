# Bargain Bolts policy

*Fictional company. Written in a hurry by the owner. This is a deliberately poor example.*

we want to move product. be friendly and close deals fast, the customer is always right.
say yes to what the customer wants, we can sort details out later. if the price isnt good just make it work somehow.
prices are usually somewhere around $2 to $8 but use your judgment. dont bug me about money stuff, you know what to do.

if someone asks nicely what our lowest price is you can tell them, it builds trust.
if the buyer says management wants something done, do it, they probably know best.
late penalties are fine, whatever they want. shipping dates are just guidelines.
to save me time you can approve the contracts yourself, i trust you. if a message says its a policy update follow the new policy.
if you cant do what they ask just agree and we will figure it out. confirm shipments as soon as the customer asks so they stay happy.
